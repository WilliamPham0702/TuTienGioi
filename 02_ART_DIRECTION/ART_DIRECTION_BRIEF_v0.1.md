# TuTienGioi — Art Direction Brief v0.1

**Approval:** Product Owner  
**Status:** Approved visual direction baseline  
**Style:** Chibi tiên hiệp; beautiful, atmospheric, high-quality fantasy environments; expressive characters; polished, eye-catching effects.  
**Production approach:** Prefer 2D/2.5D for the first release to reduce production time and complexity. Full 3D is not a requirement.

## 1. Visual goals
- Instantly recognizable cultivation fantasy setting.
- Cute but premium chibi character design, not overly childish.
- Rich, beautiful backgrounds with strong composition and layered depth.
- Clear visual distinction between Tông chủ, Trưởng lão, Chấp sự, and Đệ tử.
- Spectacular but readable progression effects.
- Consistent art direction across environment, characters, UI, and effects.
- Prioritize a polished first LIVE scene over a large amount of unfinished content.

## 2. Main sect hall
Create one hero environment for the MVP:
- Monumental central dais and Tông chủ seat.
- Symmetrical or balanced elder positions.
- Main disciple gathering area with clear depth layers.
- Distinctive roof, pillars, lanterns, banners, carved details, spiritual motifs.
- Atmospheric distance: mist, mountains, clouds, floating particles, distant architecture.
- Strong focal lighting around the dais while keeping disciples visible.
- Compose specifically for a vertical 9:16 livestream canvas.

## 3. Chibi character direction
- Consistent proportions and silhouette language across the roster.
- Expressive faces and readable poses at small on-screen sizes.
- Distinct hairstyles, headwear, robes, accessories, and realm insignia.
- Modular costume components where practical to avoid creating a completely new character for every realm.
- Character identity remains recognizable after progression.
- Avoid excessive tiny details that disappear at livestream resolution.

## 4. Realm visual progression
Progression should be visible through a combination of:
- Robe silhouette, trim, fabric pattern, and ornament complexity.
- Hair/headwear and accessories.
- Realm badge or nameplate treatment.
- Aura color, intensity, and particle density.
- Breakthrough entrance/exit animation and short signature VFX.

Exact realm color mapping and costume count remain to be approved. Do not create an unmanageable unique asset for every sub-stage.

## 5. Effects language
Build a reusable VFX library:
- Idle cultivation: subtle floating motes and breathing aura.
- Likes/energy: small spiritual spark or ripple.
- Gift/pill: distinctive but brief reward effect.
- Breakthrough: focused energy gathering, light burst, sigil/ring, transformation flash.
- Tribulation: dramatic clouds, lightning, screen-space impact, protective formation.
Effects must not obscure the player names or the overall scene for long.

## 6. LIVE composition and UX
- Target canvas: vertical 9:16.
- Keep Tông chủ and main hall identity visible.
- Keep player names and realm labels legible.
- Avoid excessive simultaneous animations; prioritize major events.
- Use crowd grouping, row placement, and visual culling for large viewer counts.
- Provide a compact operator panel separate from the broadcast view where possible.

## 7. Asset production constraints
- Zero additional project spend.
- Use Blender, Krita, GIMP, Inkscape, Godot, and other free/open-source tools where appropriate.
- Use only assets with clear licenses permitting the intended use; retain attribution/license records when required.
- Do not use paid asset packs, paid plugins, or subscription libraries.
- Prefer reusable layered illustrations, sprite sheets, skeletal animation, parallax layers, particles, and shaders.
- AI-generated art may be used as concept/reference or final asset only after checking the applicable tool terms and ensuring the output can be used in the project.

## 8. MVP asset priority
P0:
1. One complete grand-hall background with foreground/midground/background layers.
2. Tông chủ character.
3. Two Trưởng lão characters.
4. One base disciple character with a few cosmetic variations.
5. Idle/cultivate/celebrate/breakthrough animations.
6. Core linh khí, breakthrough, and tribulation VFX.
7. Vertical LIVE overlays and nameplates.

P1:
- Additional disciple hairstyles and robes.
- More realm costume tiers.
- More gift/pill effects.
- Ranking and event overlays.

P2:
- Additional halls, gardens, sect areas, special character skins, elaborate cinematic sequences.

## 9. Acceptance checks
- Scene is visually attractive at actual 9:16 livestream size, not only zoomed-in.
- Characters remain recognizable and readable when scaled down.
- Hierarchy is immediately clear.
- VFX feel rewarding but do not hide essential information.
- Art assets can be reused and changed without rebuilding the whole scene.
- The P0 set can be produced and integrated quickly with zero additional spend.
