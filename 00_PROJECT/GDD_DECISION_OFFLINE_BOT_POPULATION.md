# GDD Decision — Offline BOT & Minimum Population

## Confirmed
- Player offline: preserve their character, cultivation, items, identity, and progress; do not delete or reset it.
- Their character transitions to BOT control and continues moving/acting in the world.
- When the player returns, BOT control stops and control returns to the player on the same character.
- Maintain at least 50 active characters in the world at all times, counting real players and offline-player BOTs.
- If population falls below 50, spawn supplemental world BOT characters to fill the deficit.
- 50 is a minimum, not a cap. Do not kick or delete characters to force population down; population may exceed 50.
- If there are already 50 or more characters, do not create population filler.
- Population filler BOTs are distinct from player-owned characters converted to BOT mode.
