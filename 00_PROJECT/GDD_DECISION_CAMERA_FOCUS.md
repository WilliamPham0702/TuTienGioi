# TuTienGioi — GDD Decision: Event Camera Focus

Date: 2026-11-26
Status: CONFIRMED

## Camera focus duration by event

- Small event: 3 seconds
- Medium event: 5 seconds
- Large event: 8 seconds
- Tribulation (Độ Kiếp): 12 seconds
- Immortal Realm (Tiên Cảnh): 15 seconds
- Boss: camera focus follows battle developments; no fixed duration

## Behavior

For fixed-duration events, return to the overall gameplay view when the focus duration ends. Boss camera focus is dynamic and follows battle developments.
